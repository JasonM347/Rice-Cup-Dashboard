Ratings and name matching

Unofficial fan dashboard, not run by the Rice Cup Championships. Brackets from Challonge (thericecup.com bracket links). Ratings are CSR from the league player-stats sheet.
Model: every rating is put on the Fargo scale. CSR converts through the Midwest Poolplayers Association division table (D <1576 = <426 Fargo, C 1576 = 426, B 1651 = 476, A 1741 = 526, AA 1801 = 576, Master 1851 = 626, Pro 1950 = 730), interpolated between band edges. Fargo first (the default) uses a player's Fargo rating and falls back to converted CSR. Blend weights a player's Fargo by games/(games + 200) and their converted CSR by the rest, so a well-established Fargo dominates and a thin one barely counts. Each game uses FargoRate's odds: P = 1 / (1 + 2^((Ropp − R)/100)), so 100 points doubles the odds. A match is a race to N, and the fair race is the underdog race that brings it closest to 50/50. Title odds come from 4,000 simulated runs of the remaining bracket. Players with neither rating use the bracket median and are marked "est." Fargo ratings: FargoRate public search.
Backtest on the 75 rated Women's and Junior matches from Nov 13–14, scored at each match's real race length: Fargo first picked 51 winners (log loss 0.619), Blend 48 (0.649), and CSR via the MPA table did about the same as the earlier CSR-only model. A coin flip scores 0.693.
